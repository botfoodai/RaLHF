## What you can do (no permission needed)

- **Use the code** under the terms of Apache-2.0 — fork it, modify it, redistribute it, build commercial products on top of it.
- **Reference the project factually.** You can say "this works with RaLHF", "compatible with the RaLHF MCP server", "based on a fork of RaLHF", "inspired by RaLHF" — these are nominative uses and they are fine.
- **Describe the origin.** If you redistribute a fork, you can and should retain the copyright notices and the NOTICE file. Apache-2.0 requires it.
- **Cite the project** in articles, blog posts, talks, academic work, comparisons, and reviews — favorable or critical.
- **Say "based on RaLHF"** in a fork's README, as long as the fork itself is not branded as RaLHF.

## What you cannot do (without written permission from Bot Food Corporation)

- **Name your fork or derivative "RaLHF"** or anything confusingly similar (e.g., "RaLHF Pro", "RaLHF+", "Open RaLHF", "RaLHF Community Edition", "RaLHF X", "RaLHFv3"). Pick a different name for your fork.
- **Use "RaLHF" or "Bot Food" in your product name, company name, domain, or marketplace listing** in a way that suggests your product *is* RaLHF, comes from Bot Food Corporation, or is endorsed by Bot Food Corporation.
- **Use the RaLHF or Bot Food logos** in your own materials, even unmodified.
- **Suggest endorsement, partnership, or affiliation** that doesn't exist — including in plugin marketplaces, app stores, social media, or paid advertising.
- **Register domain names, social handles, or marketplace listings** that incorporate "RaLHF" or "Bot Food" as the primary identifier.
- **Use the marks in connection with a competing hosted/managed service** that re-implements RaLHF and presents itself as RaLHF or as a RaLHF service.

## Forks: how to do it cleanly

If you fork this project, please:

1. **Rename it.** Pick a name that doesn't include "RaLHF" or "Bot Food".
2. **Keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files intact.** Apache-2.0 requires this.
3. **Acknowledge the origin in your README.** Something like:
   > Forked from [RaLHF](https://github.com/...) by Bot Food Corporation, originally available at https://ralhf.ai. RaLHF and Bot Food are trademarks of Bot Food Corporation, used here for attribution only.
4. **Make it clear your fork is your own thing.** Don't imply it ships from us, is supported by us, or is the "official" version.

The **RaLHF backend service** at `backend.ralhf.ai`, the data pipeline, the wiki engine, and any associated infrastructure are **not covered by Apache-2.0** — they are proprietary services operated by Bot Food Corporation under separate Terms of Service available at [ralhf.ai](https://ralhf.ai).

Using a forked plugin against `backend.ralhf.ai` still requires a valid RaLHF account and compliance with the RaLHF Terms of Service.

## Requesting permission

If you have a use case that doesn't fit the rules above and you'd like permission to use the marks — for example, an integration that benefits from co-branding, a partnership, an academic project that needs the full name in a deliverable — reach out to Bot Food Corporation at the contact listed on [botfood.ai](https://botfood.ai).

We're generally friendly. The point of this policy is to keep "RaLHF" meaningful — so when a user sees the name, they know what they're getting.
